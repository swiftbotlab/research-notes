Ten Steps from 2 a.m. to Paid

I. The idea
Ideas come free at 2 a.m. and leave by half past three;
a wish with nothing riding on it flutters off for free.

II. The post
So write it as a bounty: the deed, the place, the day,
then lock the ETH beside the words, where words can't walk away.

III. The pot
Now it's a contract, not a mood. The pot sits in plain sight,
a little lighthouse on the chain that blinks all through the night.

IV. The stranger
And someone reads it on a bus, or waiting for a train,
and thinks: I could do that. And checks the forecast for the rain.

V. The deed
They go. They climb, or cook, or teach, or busk before a crowd,
and somewhere in the middle of it, lift the phone up proud.

VI. The rule
Because the rule is older than the chain, and twice as plain:
pics or it didn't happen. Without proof, a deed's a rumour in the rain.

VII. The claim
The claim goes in: a photo and a sentence, nothing more.
A stranger's "trust me" turns to "look", and look is what it's for.

VIII. The vote
The ones who funded it now vote on what the picture shows;
they judge the proof and not the face, the frame and not the pose.

IX. The payout
The vote clears; escrow lets it go; a stranger's balance grows.
The idea is an action now, and paid, and the chain knows.

X. October
October: month ten, stanza ten. The nights are getting long.
You said pics or it didn't happen, so here's the picture of this song.
